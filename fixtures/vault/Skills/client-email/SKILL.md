# Client email

- Read the person's note in People/ first (preferences, open threads).
- Keep to 3–6 sentences. No exclamation marks, no emojis.
- Put the answer in the first sentence; details after.
- Money: always state the total and what it includes.
- Save as a Gmail draft. Sending always needs the owner's approval.
